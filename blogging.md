# blog post guidelines

You'll be asked to write four brief (500-800 word) responses this term. In most cases, I'll provide a prompt to guide your writing/thinking. There's no right/wrong way to handle this responsiblity, exactly, but here are a few pointers and an example:

* **have a focus**: there is often a lot of material to digest in a week, and the best responses leave most of the material out, drawing a tight focus on a single issue that links texts or a single text/object. I don't expect a formal, thesis-driven essay with intro and conclusion, and I do want to see a rather spontaneous exercise in thinking your way thorugh an issue, but having a clear through-line is essential.
* **be specific**: I'd like to see some quotation from the text/s you're analyzing, or, for more visual kinds of content, some specific reference to the object in question. Analyze, in other words, don't summarize.
* **use the affordances of WordPress** when it makes sense: feel free to link to other essays or content, to peers' posts, or to ... anything at all that enhances your argument. You're also encouraged, though not required, to comment on each other's posts.

Lastly, [here's an example](https://dh780fa25.commons.gc.cuny.edu/2025/11/03/instagrams-forgotten-achievements-tab/) of a successful post from a very different class. Your results may vary (and they should), but I thought it might be helpful to have an example.
